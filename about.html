About DriveEase

DriveEase is an educational prototype designed to make basic car rental information easy to find and understand.

Click the car illustration to visit About Us (HTML image map).

The problem

Visitors comparing rental cars may struggle to find understandable vehicle categories, example prices, and a clear starting point. Scattered information can make simple decisions harder.

Our solution

DriveEase presents vehicle categories in a table, provides a rental cost estimator, and organizes key pages into a consistent navigation menu.

Project goals

  • Make car categories easy to compare.
  • Provide clear navigation between pages.
  • Demonstrate accessible forms and client-side validation.
  • Use responsive HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

How it works

  1. Visit the vehicle comparison table.
  2. Use the cost estimator.
  3. Explore the sample registration form.

Note: This is a static academic demonstration. It does not provide real bookings, account storage, or payment processing.
